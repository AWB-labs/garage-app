Garagey

Every car you own, and everything you have done to it.

A maintenance log that lives on your phone. Log a service in seconds, get told what is due before it is overdue, and keep the receipts, the faults and the small things you always forget in one place.

What it does

Service history

Every oil change, filter, brake job and tire, with date, mileage, cost, shop and photos of the receipt.

Reminders that follow the car

Set an interval by distance, by time, or both. Garagey works out what is upcoming, due soon, or overdue from your actual history.

Issues

Log a fault while it is fresh, rate how bad it is, and close it out with the service that fixed it.

Costs

What this car has cost you, by category and by year, in your own currency.

Notes

Tire pressures, insurance renewal, wheel bolt torque, where the locking key lives. The things that never fit anywhere else.

More than one car

A garage, not a single car app. Switch between them, and share one with a partner, a mechanic, or whoever drives it.

Built to work offline

  • Your phone is the source of truth. Every screen reads from local storage, so nothing waits on a network. Underground car park, tunnel, aeroplane: it all still works.
  • An account is optional. Without one, Garagey never touches the internet. With one, your garage follows you to your other devices and can be shared.
  • Photos stay on your device. Receipt and issue photos are never uploaded, even when you are signed in.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. There is no advertising or analytics code in the app at all.
