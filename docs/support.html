Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Garagey works completely offline and an account is optional. You only need one if you want your garage on more than one device, or you want to share a car with someone.

My garage is empty on a new phone

Records only follow you to another device if you were signed in when you made them. Sign in with the same account and the garage will sync down. Photos are the exception: they stay on the device that took them and are never uploaded.

A reminder is showing the wrong due point

Reminders are worked out from your service history, not from the day you created the rule. Log the service that actually satisfies it, with its real date and mileage, and the reminder moves to match. If nothing in your history matches the rule, it keeps the point you set when you created it.

My car shows a drawing instead of a photo

That is the default. Under Settings, Car imagery, choose Demo to fetch a studio render from make, model and year. Demo renders arrive watermarked because they use the image provider's public key. Your own key from imagin.studio renders them clean, and Drawn keeps the app fully offline.

How do I delete my account or my data?

Deleting a car removes it and its history from your devices and your account. To delete the account itself, or to ask for a copy of what is stored for it, open an issue using the button above and we will action it.

Someone shared a car with me and I cannot see it

Sharing needs an internet connection on both sides, and the invitation is matched to the exact email address it was sent to. Make sure you signed in with that address, and that you are online, then pull down to refresh.
